What happens to what you tell us. Short, because we collect very little.

Privacy · prototype terms; Pal-Online sets the final ones

What we collect

Only what you type into Tell us your work: your trade, your town or camp, what changed, what you are paid if you choose to say, and a number or address for the reporter.

This prototype sends nothing. The form checks what you type and stops there.

Who sees it

The reporter who calls you back, and the editor who checks the story. Your number or address is never published, and your name is changed on request and marked as changed.

How long we keep it

[Retention period, to be set by Pal-Online.] You can ask at any time for your contact details to be deleted and for your story to be taken down.

What this site stores in your browser

One note for the length of your visit: that you have already seen the opening animation, so it does not play again. Nothing else. No cookies are set, and the prototype loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts; its fonts are served from this site, not a third party. [Analytics on the live site: to be decided.]
